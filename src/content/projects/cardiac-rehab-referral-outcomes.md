Patients referred to cardiac rehabilitation within four weeks of an acute coronary event are
believed to do better than those referred later, but the local data has never been looked at.
This project builds a retrospective cohort of post-MI and post-PCI patients over the last
three years, comparing readmission and mortality outcomes between early and late rehabilitation
referral, using data already captured in routine clinical records.

You'll learn how to build a structured cohort from a hospital database, work with a REDCap
project set up specifically for this study, and get a practical introduction to survival
analysis — skills that transfer directly to almost any clinical research project. Weekly
check-ins with the cardiology research group keep the project on track and give you exposure
to how a research team actually runs.

Expected output is a conference abstract for a state or national cardiology meeting, with a
realistic pathway to a full manuscript if the results are interesting and you want to stay
involved beyond the initial term. Suitable for a JMO or advanced trainee wanting a substantial,
CV-building project rather than a quick audit.
