Same-day cancellations on the elective surgery list are frustrating for patients and costly for
the hospital, and the reasons behind them are rarely captured systematically. This project maps
the current pre-admission pathway end-to-end, identifies the most common preventable causes of
cancellation, and runs a series of PDSA cycles testing small changes to the pre-admission
clinic process.

You'll get structured experience in quality improvement methodology — process mapping, PDSA
cycles, run charts — plus practical experience interviewing pre-admission nurses, anaesthetic
staff and surgical booking clerks to understand where the pathway actually breaks down. This is
QI work with a genuine operational stakeholder audience, not just a data exercise.

Expected output is a presentation to the surgical department's morbidity and quality meeting,
with a written report suitable for a hospital-wide quality improvement showcase or conference.
Because this is an ongoing service improvement initiative, there's flexibility to join for one
term or stay involved across several as PDSA cycles continue.
