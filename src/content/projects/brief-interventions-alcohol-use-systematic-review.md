Brief interventions delivered by non-specialist staff are widely recommended for hospital
inpatients with risky alcohol use, but the evidence for which approaches actually change
outcomes in a general hospital (rather than a specialist addiction service) setting is
scattered. This project runs a formal systematic review following PRISMA methodology,
screening the literature and synthesising what's actually been shown to work.

You'll learn to design and run a structured search strategy across PubMed and Embase, apply
consistent inclusion/exclusion criteria across a full-text screening process, and manage a
reference library properly using EndNote or Zotero — core skills for any future evidence-based
practice or guideline work, not just this project.

Expected output is a manuscript suitable for submission to a general medicine or addiction
medicine journal, with named authorship for everyone who completes a meaningful share of the
screening and writing. This is a rigorous, longer-term project best suited to a JMO or trainee
who can commit across more than one rotation; recruitment for this round has now closed.
