High-flow nasal cannula oxygen has become common in bronchiolitis management, but its use
varies noticeably between clinicians and shifts. This project writes up a small series of
cases from the last respiratory season that illustrate that variation, paired with a focused
literature review of the current evidence and guideline recommendations.

You'll learn how to construct a case report from raw clinical notes into a de-identified,
publication-ready format, run a structured literature search, and get first-hand experience
preparing a low-risk ethics/governance submission — a genuinely useful skill for any future
research you do. Supervision is close and hands-on given the short timeframe.

Expected output is a submission to a paediatrics or emergency medicine special-interest
newsletter or regional conference, with first-author opportunity for the JMO or student who
sees it through. This listing has now closed to new expressions of interest for this rotation.
