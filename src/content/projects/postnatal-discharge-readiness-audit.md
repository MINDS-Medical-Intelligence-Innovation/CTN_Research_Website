The maternity unit introduced a structured postnatal discharge readiness checklist last year,
but nobody has formally checked how consistently it's actually being completed and documented.
This audit reviews a sample of postnatal records to measure completion rates, looks for
patterns by shift and ward area, and compares outcomes for patients where the checklist was and
wasn't fully completed.

You'll build practical chart-review and data-extraction skills, put together descriptive
statistics and simple, clear charts to present findings back to the midwifery team, and get
direct exposure to how nursing and midwifery quality data is collected and used to drive
practice change on the ward.

Expected output is a short written report and a presentation at a ward or unit in-service, with
the option to develop it into a submission for a nursing and midwifery research conference.
Well suited to a nursing or midwifery student, or a JMO on a maternity rotation, looking for a
practical first research experience.
