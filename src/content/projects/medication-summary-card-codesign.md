Older inpatients on five or more medications frequently leave hospital unsure which of their
usual medications changed, and why. This project works with patients, ward pharmacists and
nursing staff to co-design a simple, patient-held summary card that can travel with the patient
between hospital and community care, then user-tests a first draft on the general medicine
wards.

You'll run short structured interviews with patients and staff, learn the basics of
plain-language health communication, and take a design through a few rapid rounds of
prototyping and feedback — a different flavour of research skill set than a standard audit,
and a good fit if you're interested in health communication or implementation work.

Expected output is a working draft of the summary card ready for a formal pilot, plus a short
write-up suitable for a quality improvement or patient safety conference. A good option for a
student or JMO who wants a creative, patient-facing project over a single term.
