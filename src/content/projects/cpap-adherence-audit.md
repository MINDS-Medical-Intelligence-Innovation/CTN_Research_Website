CPAP is the first-line treatment for moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnoea, but real-world
adherence is notoriously patchy. This audit reviews machine-reported usage data and clinic
follow-up records for patients started on CPAP through the sleep service over the past two
years, and benchmarks local adherence rates against the published literature.

You'll get hands-on experience with structured chart review, learn how to design and administer
a short patient survey to understand *why* people stop using their machine, and put together
basic descriptive statistics and simple charts to summarise the findings — a well-scoped, very
achievable first audit if you haven't done one before.

The project is designed to wrap up within a single term and produce a short written report for
the sleep service, with the option to submit the findings as a poster at a respiratory or sleep
medicine conference. Well suited to a student or early JMO looking for a manageable introduction
to clinical research.
