Ellery coordinates recruitment, consent and follow-up logistics for clinical trials running
across several departments at Campbelltown Hospital, and is often the first person a new
student or JMO speaks to when working out how a trial-based project actually runs day to day.

Ellery also manages the hospital's site-level research governance paperwork and is a useful
contact for anyone whose project involves human research ethics applications or site
governance authorisation for the first time — happy to walk newcomers through what's actually
involved before it feels overwhelming.
