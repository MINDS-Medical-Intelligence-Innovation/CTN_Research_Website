Priya is a staff specialist cardiologist at Campbelltown Hospital with an interest in what
happens to patients *after* the acute admission — particularly how quickly and how well people
get into cardiac rehabilitation, and whether that timing changes longer-term outcomes. She
supervises retrospective and audit-style projects for students and JMOs most terms and is
happy to hear from anyone interested in cardiovascular health services research.

Outside clinical and research work, Priya is part of the hospital's cardiac rehabilitation
governance group and has an ongoing interest in mentoring junior doctors into their first
research project.
