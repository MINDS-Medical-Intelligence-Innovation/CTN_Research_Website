Sam is a senior physiotherapist and allied health researcher whose work looks at how early
mobilisation and structured rehabilitation programs affect functional outcomes and length of
stay for older inpatients. Sam completed a PhD examining falls prevention interventions and
now supervises allied health students and JMOs on quality improvement and prospective projects.

A strong advocate for allied health voices in hospital research more broadly, Sam co-chairs the
hospital's allied health research interest group, which meets monthly and welcomes anyone
curious about research from a physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech pathology or
dietetics background.
