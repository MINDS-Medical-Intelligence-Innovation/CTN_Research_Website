Morgan is the Clinical Nurse Consultant supporting nursing and midwifery research across
Campbelltown Hospital, helping ward-based staff turn everyday clinical questions into properly
scoped audits and quality improvement projects. Current work focuses on postnatal discharge
processes and how well documented practice actually matches what happens at the bedside.

Morgan runs a monthly drop-in session for nurses, midwives and students who have an idea for a
project but aren't sure where to start, and is a good first point of contact for anyone in
nursing or midwifery wanting to get involved in research for the first time.
