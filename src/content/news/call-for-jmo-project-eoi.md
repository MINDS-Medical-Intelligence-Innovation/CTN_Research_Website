With a new rotation about to start, now is a good time to line up a research project. The
[Students & JMOs](/students/) noticeboard currently lists eight projects spanning Cardiology,
Respiratory & Sleep, Paediatrics, Emergency, General Medicine, Mental Health, Surgery, and
Nursing & Midwifery research — everything from a one-term audit you can finish before your
next rotation, to an ongoing quality-improvement project you can stay involved with across
several terms.

Each listing shows the department, the supervisor, the type of project (audit, QI, case
report, retrospective or prospective study, systematic review), the realistic time commitment,
and the skills you'll come away with — so you can find something that actually fits your
rotation rather than guessing. Listings marked "Recruiting students now" have a supervisor
ready to take you on immediately; others may still be worth an email even without the badge.

If something catches your eye, read the supervisor's recent publications on the
[publications wall](/publications/) so you arrive at that first conversation with a sense of
what the group is currently working on, then send a short, specific email — who you are, your
availability, and why the project interests you — and submit an expression of interest so the
research office can help match you if your first choice fills up.
