The [publications page](/publications/) is now backed by an automated weekly pipeline rather
than a manually maintained list. Every Saturday morning, the site searches PubMed for papers
carrying a Campbelltown Hospital affiliation, retrieves the title, authors, journal and DOI,
and tags each paper by department based on the author affiliations recorded on the article
itself. New papers are added automatically.

To distinguish papers with a contributing Campbelltown author from Campbelltown-led research,
papers with a Campbelltown-based first, last or corresponding author carry a distinct
**"Campbelltown-led"** badge and can be filtered separately on the
[Research](/research/) and publications pages. The site's statistics report both counts
separately.

The same automated approach now covers the [Clinical Trials](/trials/) page, which syncs
recruiting-trial listings from ClinicalTrials.gov. Every update arrives as a reviewable change
in the site's history rather than a silent edit — nothing is published automatically without
a clear audit trail. Researcher profiles remain human-gated: a profile is only published once
the person it belongs to has confirmed they wish to appear.
