The [publications wall](/publications/) is now backed by an automated weekly pipeline rather
than a hand-maintained list. Every Saturday morning, the site searches PubMed for papers
carrying a Campbelltown Hospital affiliation, pulls in the title, authors, journal and DOI,
and tags each paper by department based on the author affiliations recorded on the article
itself. New papers simply appear — nobody has to remember to add them.

To keep the wall honest about the difference between "a Campbelltown author contributed" and
"this is Campbelltown-led research", papers with a Campbelltown-based first, last or
corresponding author carry a distinct **"Campbelltown-led"** badge and can be filtered
separately on the [Research](/research/) and publications pages. The stat tiles on the site
report both counts, so the headline publication number and the "led from here" number are
never conflated.

The same automate-first approach now covers the [Clinical Trials](/trials/) page, which syncs
recruiting-trial listings from ClinicalTrials.gov. Every update arrives as a reviewable change
in the site's history rather than a silent edit — nothing is auto-published without a clear
audit trail, in line with how the project's pipelines are designed to work. Researcher
profiles remain the one part of this system that stays firmly human-gated: a profile only goes
live once the person it belongs to has actively confirmed they want to appear.
