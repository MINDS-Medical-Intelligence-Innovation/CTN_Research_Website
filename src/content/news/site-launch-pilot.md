author: "Research Website Team"
---

This site is a pilot project built by clinicians and researchers at Campbelltown Hospital to solve
a simple problem: research activity here was scattered across PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov,
email threads and personal knowledge, with no single public home.

The publications wall and clinical trials listing update automatically from PubMed and
ClinicalTrials.gov. Researcher profiles are added with consent. The **Students & JMOs**
section exists to make it easier for junior doctors and students to find a project, a
supervisor, and a research community — something no other South Western Sydney resource
currently offers.

This is an independent research-community initiative, not an official SWSLHD website. We're
building it in the open and would welcome feedback from anyone at Campbelltown who wants to
get involved.
